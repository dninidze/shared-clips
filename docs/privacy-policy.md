# ClipSweep privacy policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

ClipSweep is an Android app that moves videos you saved from apps like TikTok out of your phone's
Camera folder, so your photo backup does not upload them, and can delete them after a number of
days you choose.

## What the app accesses

- **Videos on your phone.** ClipSweep reads the list of videos in your Camera folder and their
  details (file name, size, date, which app saved them, and video metadata such as whether a camera
  recorded it) to recognize saved TikTok videos. It moves the ones it recognizes to the
  "Movies/TikTok Downloads" folder, and moves them to the system trash when your auto-delete setting
  says so.
- **Media location.** Android requires this access before an app may manage media without asking
  for confirmation each time. ClipSweep does not read or use location data from your videos.
- **Notifications.** Used only to tell you about new videos to move or old ones to delete.

## What the app does not do

- ClipSweep does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.
- It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service.
- Your videos never leave your phone because of ClipSweep. The app has no internet permission.

## Data kept on your phone

ClipSweep keeps its settings and a short activity log (what it moved or deleted, and when) in its
own private storage on your phone. The log only leaves your phone if you choose "Share log". Clearing
the app's data or uninstalling it removes all of it.

## Children

ClipSweep is not directed at children under 13.
